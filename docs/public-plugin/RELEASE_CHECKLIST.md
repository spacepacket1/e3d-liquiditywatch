## What "no legal documents drafted without approval" actually meant

The original constraint was never touch real legal text without the owner in the loop —
not "never touch it at all." In practice: this session asked the owner directly for the
facts and legal choices it couldn't infer (retention period, email processor, analytics
tool, jurisdiction, dispute resolution, age restriction), drafted real policy/terms text
from those answers, showed the complete text for review, and only published after an
explicit "yes, publish it." Nothing was published unreviewed or guessed. If
LiquidityWatch's scope or risk profile changes materially, these should get a real legal
review rather than another quick session-driven pass — see `PRIVACY_REVIEW.md`'s closing
caveat.

  everything up to that point and confirmed results afterward, never acted in their
  account itself.
- The existing private ChatGPT connection was never taken down; every production change
  was verified live against the same URL it already uses, both before and after
  submission.
