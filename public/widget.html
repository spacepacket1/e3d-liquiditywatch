Embed the Stress Score

A live gauge of the U.S. Financial Stress Score for your blog, newsletter archive or dashboard. Free to use; it links back to LiquidityWatch.

Live preview

Copy and paste

Add this wherever you want the gauge to appear:

<div data-liquiditywatch></div>
<script async src="https://liquiditywatch.e3d.ai/widget.js"></script>

Prefer no script? Use the frame directly:

<iframe src="https://liquiditywatch.e3d.ai/embed.html" title="LiquidityWatch: U.S. Financial Stress Score" width="320" height="340" style="border:0" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Good to know

  • The widget loads the current reading each time it is viewed, so it stays up to date without any work on your side.
  • It runs inside its own frame, so it can't change your page's styles or read your page.
  • The score is produced by an AI process and is informational only. See how it works.
